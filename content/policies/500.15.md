# Residency Policy (500.15)

IECC complies with Illinois Community College Board’s Administrative Rules and the Illinois Public Community College Act in the establishment of student residency for the purpose of enrollment reporting and tuition assessment. Evidence of residency for each applicant to Illinois Eastern Community Colleges shall be provided before or at the time of registration, but no later than the first day of classes. Evidence of residency is the responsibility of the student. Procedures have been developed describing the various residency classifications which include qualifications, special provisions, and required documentation to serve as proof of residency.
