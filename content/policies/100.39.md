# Board Treasurer (100.39)

The Board of Trustees shall appoint a Treasurer who shall serve at the pleasure of the Board. The Treasurer shall not be a member of the Board, as required by the Public Community College Act 110 ILCS 805/3-18, and shall be bonded as required and in accordance with 110 ILCS 805/3-19. The Board of Trustees shall pay the premium on the bond from funds of the District.
