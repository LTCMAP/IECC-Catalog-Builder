# Instructional Materials Policy (500.6)

Definition: The term instructional materials include textbooks and other types of student materials used specifically for classroom or individualized instruction. General guidelines and procedures will be developed for adoption, purchase, buy-back, markup, and other aspects of instructional materials utilization.
