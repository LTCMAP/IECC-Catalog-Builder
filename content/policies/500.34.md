# Student Religious Observances Policy (500.34)

In compliance with the University Religious Observances Act (110 ILCS 110), Illinois Eastern Community Colleges does not discriminate against students based on religious observances. IECC will reasonably accommodate the religious observances of individual students in regard to admissions, class attendance, and the scheduling of examinations and work requirements. IECC procedures further describe expectations of the student and a student grievance process.
