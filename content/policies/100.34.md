# Employment Contract Transparency and Annual Performance Review (100.34)

Employment Effective January 1, 2017, the following policy shall apply to the employment contracts of each of the Presidents/Vice Chancellors or the Chancellor entered into, amended, renewed, or extended after the effective date. This policy does not apply to collective bargaining agreements. With respect to employment contracts entered into with any of the four Presidents/Vice Chancellors or the Chancellor:

- Severance payments or contract buyouts may be placed in an escrow account if there are pending criminal charges against a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor related to their employment.

- Final action on the formation, renewal, extension, or termination of the employment contracts of a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor must be made during an Open Meeting of the Board.

- Public notice, compliant with the Open Meetings Act, must be given prior to final action on the formation, renewal, extension, or termination of the employment contracts of a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor. In addition, any appointment of a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor must include a copy of the Board item or other documentation providing, at a minimum, a description of the proposed principal financial components of the appointment.

- Any performance-based bonus or incentive-based compensation to a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor must be approved by the Board in an Open Meeting. The performance criteria and goals upon which the bonus or incentive-based compensation is based must be made available to the public no less than 48 hours before Board approval of the performance-based bonus or incentive-based compensation.

- Board minutes, Board packets, and annual performance criteria and goals concerning a President/Vice Chancellor or the Chancellor must be made available to the public on the community college district's Internet website. Annual Performance Review The Board will complete an annual performance review of each President/Vice Chancellor and the Chancellor. An annual performance review will be considered when the Board contemplates a bonus, raise, or severance agreement for a President/Vice Chancellor or Chancellor.
