# Credit Hour Policy (800.8)

Illinois Eastern Community Colleges defines a credit hour as “An amount of work represented in intended learning outcomes and verified by evidence of student achievement that reasonably approximates not less than 45 hours of combined classroom/laboratory and study time for each semester hour.” This definition is in accordance with commonly accepted practices in higher education and ICCB Administrative Rules Section 1501.309. Courses, regardless of modality or instructional method, will be consistent in the assignment of credits, rigor, quality, assessment, and learning outcomes. Time Requirements The following illustrates the semester hours required for one credit hour.
