# Assignment of Vehicles (300.18)

Requests for IECC-owned or -leased vehicles are coordinated by the Business Office at each location. Vehicles shall be assigned to employees on a short-term basis, subject to appropriate approvals and defined processes. Assigned vehicles are to be used predominantly for official IECC business. Any personal use of vehicles will be considered a taxable fringe benefit and reported to the Internal Revenue Service in accordance with prescribed guidelines.
