# Credit Card Security Policy (100.26)

IECC is committed to the implementation of the Payment Card Industry (PCI) security policies that protect information utilized by IECC in attaining its business goals. All IECC employees are required to adhere to the policies in the PCI standard. The PCI requirements apply to all systems that store, process or transmit cardholder data. IECC’s current card holder environment consists of imprint machines, standalone dial-out terminals and limited payment applications (typically point-of-sale systems) connected to the internet. IECC’s environment does not include storage of credit holder data on any computer system. Should IECC implement additional acceptance channels, begin storing, processing, or transmitting cardholder data in an electronic format, it will be the responsibility of IECC to determine the appropriate compliance criteria and implement additional PCI policies and controls as needed. In addition, IECC shall establish and maintain a formal security awareness program to make all IECC staff aware of the importance of cardholder data security.
