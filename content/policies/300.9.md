# Imprest and Petty Cash Funds (300.9)

Imprest and/or petty cash funds shall be established as needed to make payments to vendors and suppliers in less than a 30-day period from the time of purchase. Procedures for limitations and use of such funds shall be outlined in a procedure manual.
