# Bloodborne Pathogens (100.11)

Illinois Eastern Community Colleges adopts the federal OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030 and any additions thereto. The District will publish procedures so as to prevent or minimize the occupational exposure of employees to bloodborne pathogens or other potentially infectious materials.
