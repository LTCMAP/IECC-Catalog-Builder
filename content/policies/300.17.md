# Use of Facilities (300.17)

District-owned facilities and equipment may be made available for use by outside entities, provided such use does not interfere with regular education functions and/or IECC-related activities. Regulations and limitations shall be established for such use and a procedure shall provide guidelines for usage of district property. Outside entities using IECC facilities and equipment must be responsible for damages sustained by such use and will be required to furnish/provide proof of sufficient liability insurance, as appropriate. District policies, procedures, regulations, and applicable laws must be followed. A fee schedule for use of facilities and equipment will be established and maintained up to date via recommendations presented for approval by the Board of Trustees.
