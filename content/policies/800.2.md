# Program Review and Evaluation (800.2)

A full cycle of program review and evaluation should be made every five years, to include program need, cost and quality. The review will involve faculty, and may include staff, students and administration. Advisory committees, consultants and/or other sources may be utilized when appropriate. The review should be comprehensive, systematic, and district-wide. Guidelines of external agencies may be used by the District to fulfill the requirements of these agencies.
