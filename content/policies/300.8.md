# Emergency Building Repair Fund (300.8)

The Board of Trustees has established an emergency building repair fund for the purpose of correcting construction deficiencies and/or structural deterioration. It is the responsibility of the Chancellor to determine the appropriate use of those funds and to recommend same to the Board for approval of expenditure.
