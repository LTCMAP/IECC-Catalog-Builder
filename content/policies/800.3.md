# Lower-Division Baccalaureate Courses (800.3)

Pursuant to ICCB Rule 1501.309d1, courses designed to meet lower-division baccalaureate degree requirements shall be applicable to associate transfer degrees. For each baccalaureate course offered, Illinois Eastern Community Colleges shall maintain current written articulation agreements or transfer equivalency documents with:

1. at least three (3) Illinois public universities; or

2. at least three (3) baccalaureate degree-granting institutions to which a majority (51%) of IECC students transfer;

3. one or more baccalaureate degree-granting institutions to which a majority (51%) of IECC students majoring in the field for which the course is required transfer.
