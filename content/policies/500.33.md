# Student Consumer Information Disclosure Policy (500.33)

In accordance with the U.S. Higher Education Act of 1965, as amended, and regulations defined in 34 CFR 668 Subpart D (Institutional and Financial Assistance Information for Students), Illinois Eastern Community Colleges shall make specified student consumer information available to: 1) current and prospective students. IECC complies by disseminating information that includes numerous topics, such as: financial assistance, campus security, student outcomes, and general institutional information. This information is disclosed publicly to all via the IECC website at https://www.iecc.edu/disclosures and provided to current students and employees via their official IECC email accounts in the spring and fall each year. 2) the Department of Education or other reporting agencies as required. IECC complies by providing required data in the prescribed format to the appropriate authorities by the imposed deadlines. Procedures will be developed for those disclosures that require additional guidance, guidelines, or instruction in order to meet disclosure requirements.
