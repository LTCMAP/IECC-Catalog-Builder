# Paid Administrative Leave for COVID-19 (400.29)

THIS POLICY EXPIRED 5/16/2022 As a public community college, Illinois Eastern Community Colleges is required to abide by the Public Community College Act. Public Act 102-0697 amends the Public Community College Act to provide paid leave for reasons related to COVID-19. IECC will adhere to Public Act 102-0697 and will extend the benefits to all employees regardless of vaccination status. Temporary COVID-19 Sick Pay REPEALED AND REPLACED by above, April 19, 2022 Date Adopted: January 18, 2022 Effective Date: 01/03/2022 Expiration Date: 05/16/2022 (or upon conclusion of the Pandemic by the WHO, whichever occurs first)
