# Criminal Background Checks on Students (500.22)

IECC is committed to ensuring sufficient criminal background investigations are performed on students prior to acceptance into, or participation in, applicable programs or extracurricular activities. In the absence of external agency mandates pertaining to the extensiveness of the student background check, the appropriate dean or chief academic officer will make a determination. Further, the appropriate dean or chief academic officer shall have the right and authority to initiate a more extensive background check than that mandated by the external agency.
