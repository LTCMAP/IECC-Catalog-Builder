# Identity Theft Prevention Program (100.23)

The Board of Trustees of Illinois Eastern Community College District #529 is dedicated to preventing identity theft and has implemented a comprehensive prevention program in compliance with the Federal Trade Commission’s Red Flags Rule. The Board of Trustees has delegated the operational oversight of the Program to the Identify Theft Prevention Team. The Program shall:

- include reasonable methods to identify and detect red flags associated with covered accounts;

- describe appropriate responses to detected red flags in order to prevent and mitigate identity theft;

- provide for a monitoring process to ensure the Program remains effective, updating it periodically, as necessary, to reflect change in risks or operational processes; and

- ensure adequate staff training is provided for all involved in the process.
