# State Officials and Employees Ethics Act (100.20)

The Board of Trustees and Employees of Illinois Eastern Community Colleges (IECC) shall adhere to those provisions of the State Officials and Employees Ethics Act 5 ILCS 430 (Act) that were adopted, via the Ethics Ordinance (Ordinance), by the IECC Board of Trustees on April 20, 2004.

I. Provisions of the Act

A. Prohibited Political Activity (Article 5) IECC trustees and employees shall not intentionally perform or intentionally require any other trustee or employee to perform any prohibited political activity during any compensated time. Details and exceptions to this prohibited activity are provided for in the Act/Ordinance and shall be outlined in procedures.

B. Gift Ban (Article 10) IECC trustees and employees shall not intentionally solicit or accept any gift from a prohibited source or in violation of any federal or state statute, rule, or regulation. This ban applies to and includes spouses of or an immediate family member living with the trustee or employee. (No prohibited source shall offer or make a gift that violates this section.) Details and exceptions to this ban are provided for in the Act/Ordinance and shall be outlined in procedures.

C. Ethics Officer (Article 15) The Chair of the Board of Trustees shall designate an Ethics Officer for the District. The minimum duties of the Ethics Officer shall be as provided in the Act/Ordinance:

(1) To review statements of economic interest and disclosure forms of Illinois Eastern Community Colleges Trustees and senior employees before they are filed with the Secretary of State;

(2) To provide guidance to trustees and employees in the interpretation and implementation of the Act/Ordinance and this policy.

D. Ethics Commission (Article 20) The Chancellor, with the advice and consent of the Board of Trustees, shall appoint an Ethics Commission to investigate written complaints alleging a violation of this Ordinance. Procedures will further describe additional duties of the Commission and describe, at a minimum, the complaint, investigation, and hearing processes.

E. Penalties (Article 25) Penalties have been established per the Ordinance and are outlined in procedures. II. Training The Executive Director of Human Resources is charged with ensuring employees receive appropriate ethics and conduct training. The Board of Trustees receive ethics training in compliance with The Public Community College Act.
