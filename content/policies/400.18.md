# Oral English Language Proficiency (400.18)

Procedures shall be established to ensure that every full- and part-time faculty member is orally proficient in the English language prior to providing any classroom instruction to students. This policy meets the requirements of Section 3-29.2 of the Illinois Public Community College Act.
