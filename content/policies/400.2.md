# Evaluation Policy (400.2)

Procedures shall be established for the comprehensive evaluation of employee performance.

Faculty

1. Tenured faculty will be evaluated a minimum of every two academic years.

2. Full-time probationary faculty will be evaluated at least once an academic year.

3. Part-time faculty will be evaluated during the first semester of employment and a minimum of once every two academic years of teaching thereafter. Staff

1. Full and part-time administrative, professional/non-faculty, technical, clerical/secretarial, and maintenance/custodial employees will be evaluated ninety days following their initial employment. The goal of the ninety-day evaluation is to ensure a high level of performance by promoting meaningful communication between the supervisor and the employee at first hire. A yearly evaluation will be conducted after the initial employment year.

2. Student workers do not need to be formally evaluated, but should be monitored appropriately during their employment.
