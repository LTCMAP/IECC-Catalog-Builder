# Prohibiting Sex Discrimination (100.42)

By Order of the U.S. Dept of Education Office for Civil Rights, the 2024 Title IX Rule has been invalidated and the 2020 Title IX Rule is in effect, retroactively August 1, 2024 (This action invalidates Prohibiting Sex Discrimination Policy and Procedure 100.42; Ref. Preventing Sexual Misconduct Policy 100.31, which has been reinstated.)
