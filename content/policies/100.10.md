# Chronic Communicable Disease Policy (100.10)

Any case of communicable disease reported to the administration will be investigated. Appropriate action will be taken to protect students and personnel on the basis of qualified medical advice. Contractors to District 529 will be expected to cooperate in implementation of this policy.
